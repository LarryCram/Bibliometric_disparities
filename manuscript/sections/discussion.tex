\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Corpus Definition Boundaries in Olensky's Data Accuracy Framework}
Olensky established that bibliometric data accuracy is governed by three hierarchical tiers: transcription errors within raw source records, algorithmic failure during entity disambiguation and citation matching, and structural divergence in corpus definition boundaries \cite{olenskyDataAccuracyBibliometric2015,olenskyEvaluationCitationMatching2016}. While algorithmic matching in modern open infrastructures achieves high recall when identifiers are present \cite{priemOpenAlexFullyopenIndex2022}, our empirical findings demonstrate that the dominant source of bibliometric disparity is structural corpus boundary divergence. 

The divergence between the ERA 2018 submission dataset (401,261 works) and the OpenAlex Australian address-defined corpus (744,574 works) does not represent database indexing omissions or bibliographic matching error. Rather, it reflects the mathematical confrontation between two fundamentally distinct ontological entities: an address-based production model and a census-based workforce evaluation model.

\subsection{Workforce Portability and the Temporal Mobility Gradient}
National evaluation frameworks assess the retrospective productivity of eligible academic staff employed at a fixed administrative census date (31 March 2017 for ERA 2018). Under ARC portability provisions, researchers who change institutions prior to the census date submit their complete six-year retrospective publication portfolio under their new employing institution, regardless of where the research was performed or funded \cite{mowBlackBoxResearch2010}.

This administrative mechanism generates the monotonic temporal gradient observed in Table~\ref{tab:temporal_gradient}. The probability that an author was employed at their census-date institution decays monotonically with temporal distance from the census date. Consequently, works published in 2011 exhibit a 19.44\% pure foreign affiliation rate and a 20.07\% inter-institutional domestic divergence rate, meaning that 39.51\% of submitted 2011 outputs carry no published affiliation with the university claiming credit for the work. University rankings that aggregate citations from address-indexed databases measure geographical site production, whereas national evaluation exercises measure the mobile intellectual capital accumulated at a single census date \cite{vanraanFatalAttractionConceptual2005}.

\subsection{Trainee Authorship and Pre-Census Attrition}
Doctoral and postdoctoral researchers author substantial volumes of high-impact scholarship within university laboratories. ERA eligibility regulations restrict submission rights to staff holding formal continuing or fixed-term academic appointments of at least 0.4 FTE at the census date \cite{mowBlackBoxResearch2010}. 

When graduate researchers complete their candidatures and transition to industry, clinical practice, or overseas postdocs prior to the census date, their publications become administratively unclaimable by the host institution. This mechanism accounts for the omission of foundational computational algorithms developed by enrolled doctoral students (such as \textit{Grey Wolf Optimizer}, cited over 19,000 times), despite explicit university affiliations on the published records.

\subsection{Independent Research Institutes and External Payroll Boundaries}
In biomedical and physical sciences, extensive research activity is situated within independent Medical Research Institutes (MRIs) and statutory government agencies (e.g., CSIRO, ANSTO). Authors affiliated with these organizations routinely maintain adjunct or honorary university titles. 

ARC guidelines prohibit universities from claiming outputs authored by honorary staff unless the university paid a substantive academic salary or established audited joint-appointment cost-sharing contracts \cite{mowBlackBoxResearch2010}. Consequently, landmark biomedical software contributions (exemplified by \textit{limma}, with over 40,000 citations) are excluded from university submissions despite explicitly displaying university departmental addresses on the publication masthead.

\subsection{Internal Citation Integration and Copied Reference Dynamics}
The unsubmitted corpus is not scientifically marginal. ERA-submitted publications generate 109,443 direct citation links into 45,822 unsubmitted Australian works. Within academic citation networks, highly cited papers propagate through secondary citation copying without independent verification, magnifying visibility disparities between submitted and omitted literature \cite{simkinCopiedCitationsCreate2003,simkinReadYouCite2002}. When performance evaluations restrict citation baselines to submitted sets, they truncate the empirical reference graph, systematically distorting disciplinary field-normalized benchmarks \cite{hicksBibliometricsLeidenManifesto2015,mitchellAffiliationErrorsDistorted2026}.
