\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

\subsection{The Inclusion--Omission Landscape}

Between 2011 and 2016, OpenAlex indexes 744,574 distinct scholarly outputs carrying an author affiliation associated with at least one of the 40 Australian universities. Table~\ref{tab:inclusion_landscape} partitions this address-defined corpus by ERA 2018 submission status.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Comparative Profile of Australian University Publications in OpenAlex (2011--2016) by ERA Submission Status.}
\label{tab:inclusion_landscape}
\begin{tabular*}{\textwidth}{@{\extracolsep{\fill}}lrrrrrr@{}}
\toprule
Status & Total Works & Share (\%) & Mean Cites & Median Cites & Has DOI (\%) & Paratext (\%) \\
\midrule
In ERA Corpus       & 401,261 & 53.9\% & 66.4 & 28.0 & 96.8\% & 0.02\% \\
Not in ERA Corpus   & 343,313 & 46.1\% & 19.4 &  2.0 & 90.5\% & 0.69\% \\
\midrule
Total Australian HEP & 744,574 & 100.0\% & 44.7 & 11.0 & 93.9\% & 0.33\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular*}
\end{table}

Of the 744,574 address-attributed outputs, 401,261 works (53.9\%) appear in the ERA submission dataset, while 343,313 works (46.1\%) were omitted from submission by all Australian institutions. Submitted works display a median citation count of 28.0 ($\mu = 66.4$), whereas unsubmitted works exhibit a median citation count of 2.0 ($\mu = 19.4$). Within the unsubmitted partition, 41.3\% of works record zero or one citation.

For book chapters and complete books, the proportion submitted to ERA drops to 25.0\% and 22.2\% respectively.

\subsection{Institutional Attribution Discordance and the Temporal Mobility Gradient}

Evaluating institutional attribution across 363,100 ERA canonical works possessing author affiliation strings reveals systemic divergence between the submitting institution and the published affiliation. Table~\ref{tab:affiliation_concordance} details this distribution.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Concordance Between ERA Submitting Institution and Published Affiliations Across 363,100 Linked Works.}
\label{tab:affiliation_concordance}
\begin{tabular*}{\textwidth}{@{\extracolsep{\fill}}lrr@{}}
\toprule
Affiliation Classification on Published Work & Total Works & Share (\%) \\
\midrule
Submitting HEP Printed on Work                      & 245,601 & 67.64\% \\
Submitting HEP Absent, but Other Australian Entity Present &  67,537 & 18.60\% \\
No Australian Entity on Work (Pure Foreign Affiliation) &  49,962 & 13.76\% \\
\midrule
Total Evaluated Works                              & 363,100 & 100.00\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular*}
\end{table}

The submitting university appears in the author affiliation block in 245,601 works (67.64\%). In 67,537 works (18.60\%), the submitting HEP is absent while another Australian institutional entity is present. In 49,962 works (13.76\%), the publication contains zero Australian author affiliations, listing foreign institutions exclusively.

Table~\ref{tab:temporal_gradient} disaggregates institutional attribution concordance by publication year across the six-year retrospective assessment window.

Attribution concordance displays a strict monotonic temporal trajectory. For publications in 2016 (one year prior to census), 74.58\% carry the submitting HEP affiliation and 8.40\% carry purely foreign affiliations. For publications in 2011 (six years prior to census), the submitting HEP share drops to 60.49\% while the pure foreign share increases to 19.44\%, representing a 2.31-fold expansion in non-domestic attribution.
